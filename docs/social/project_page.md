**The blurb, if it wants refreshing (the current one is fine):**

A Halloween prop for the front porch. Somebody walks toward the door, a 24 GHz mmWave human presence radar sees them coming, and a foam spider drops out of the dark, half a meter to a bit over a meter, timed to land about when they reach the door. Then a stepper motor winds it back up until a bead on the line clicks a switch, a finger locks the spool, and everything shuts off; it holds with no power. Over 500 drops so far. Build one from GitHub: every printed part is Python code, the firmware runs on an ESP32, and the build guide is one page.
