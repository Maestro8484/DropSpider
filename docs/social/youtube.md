**Title (under 100 characters):**

DropSpider: a porch spider that drops on whoever walks up, then climbs back up and waits

**Description:**

Somebody walks toward the door, a mmWave radar on the device sees them coming, and a foam spider drops out of the dark, timed to land about when they get there. Then a stepper motor winds it back up until a bead on the line clicks a switch, a finger locks the spool, and everything shuts off. It holds with no power.

Over 500 drops so far, each one winding home and locking. The second angle is the doorbell camera.

Build one, all of it free on GitHub: 3D printed parts (every part is Python code), the wiring, the firmware, a one-page build guide, and the parts list with links.

GitHub: https://github.com/Maestro8484/DropSpider
Parts: https://github.com/Maestro8484/DropSpider/blob/master/docs/parts.md
Project page: https://theschmidtwithin.com/bench/#dropspider

What's in it: ESP32 DevKit V1, TMC2209 stepper driver, NEMA 11 stepper, SG90 servo, HF0612 one-way needle bearing, DFRobot C4002 24 GHz mmWave radar, KW12-3 roller switch, 3D printed bracket and spool in PLA. Bonded nylon upholstery thread for the line (its stretch is the shock absorber).
